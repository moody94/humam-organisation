Frequently asked questions

Questions? Start here.

Can I join the MEAL Bridge Community without working for MEAL Bridge?

Yes. Community membership is a distinct pathway and does not require employment or an internship with MEAL Bridge.

Can interns and employees also participate in the Community?

Yes. Community participation can continue alongside either an internship or employment with MEAL Bridge.

Can I be an intern and an employee at the same time?

No. Internships and employment are distinct engagement arrangements.

Does an internship guarantee employment?

No. Internships provide learning and practical experience, while employment depends on available opportunities, requirements, performance, and the applicable selection process.

Does Community membership guarantee access to jobs, internships, or Professional Practices?

No. Community membership may help you discover and prepare for opportunities, but each pathway has its own eligibility and selection requirements.

Can my pathway change over time?

Yes. You may pursue different opportunities over time, subject to the requirements of each pathway.

What if there are no open positions right now?

You can still join the MEAL Bridge Community, explore available internships, and stay connected with future opportunities.

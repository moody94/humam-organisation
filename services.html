FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to Common Questions

Here are some of the questions organizations frequently ask before engaging with MEAL Bridge LLC.

What types of organizations do you work with?

We work with NGOs, INGOs, UN agencies, government institutions, universities, donors, foundations, and social enterprises seeking stronger MEAL systems and organizational performance.

Can services be delivered remotely?

Yes. Most consulting, technical support, coaching, mentoring, and training services can be delivered remotely. We also provide on-site support where appropriate.

Do you provide customized solutions?

Absolutely. Every organization has different needs. We tailor our consulting, training, and technical support to your objectives, context, and operational environment.

Can you support emergency and humanitarian programmes?

Yes. We have experience supporting humanitarian, recovery, resilience, and development programmes, including work in fragile and conflict-affected contexts.

How do we request a proposal?

Contact us through our website or email with a brief description of your needs. We'll arrange an initial discussion before preparing a tailored technical and financial proposal.

Do you offer long-term partnerships?

Yes. In addition to short-term assignments, we offer ongoing technical support through retainer agreements, long-term capacity development, and strategic partnerships.

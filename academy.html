Progressive professional development

Start at the right level. Build toward the next one.

The Academy is organized around five stages of capability, making it easier to choose learning that matches your current role and professional direction.

  1. 01 Level 1

    Explore

    Enter the profession, understand MEAL, and plan your development.

  2. 02 Level 2

    Practice

    Build the core technical competencies required for practical MEAL work.

  3. 03 Level 3

    Design

    Design and strengthen systems, functions, data products, and workflows.

  4. 04 Level 4

    Specialize

    Develop advanced expertise for complex methods and operating contexts.

  5. 05 Level 5

    Lead

    Manage MEAL teams and influence evidence-based organizational decisions.
